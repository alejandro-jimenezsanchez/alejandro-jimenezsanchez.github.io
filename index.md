# Alejandro Jimenez, PhD
**Principal Data Scientist | Immunotherapy Platform, James P. Allison Institute | MD Anderson Cancer Center**


I lead the Data Science team at the Allison Institute, where we integrate computation, biology, and translation to understand how cancer evolves — and how the immune system responds.


## Research Vision {#research-vision}
We view **metastasis as a system** — an interconnected, adaptive process rather than a linear cascade. Our group develops integrative frameworks combining single-cell, spatial, and genomic data to map tumor–immune ecosystems and uncover molecular programs driving plasticity and therapy response.


### Current Focus
- Data integration across single-cell, spatial, and bulk modalities
- Computational modeling of plasticity and immune evasion
- Translational data science for immunotherapy development
- Open-source tools and reproducible pipelines


## Our Team {#our-team}
The Data Science group at the Allison Institute builds computational foundations for immunotherapy discovery — bridging clinical data, high-dimensional assays, and mechanistic modeling.


- **Mission:** Turn complexity into insight, and insight into translational impact.
- **Values:** Rigor × creativity, openness, reproducibility, collaborative science.
